COHERENT CAPITAL

Business Growth Plan — 2025–2026

Next Review Date: __________________________

1. Description of Expansion Opportunities

Coherent Capital will expand its digital services by integrating targeted AI tools for social media content creation and scheduling, automated code assistance for web development, SEO optimisation workflows, and streamlined UI/UX design systems. This reduces the load on the sole developer, accelerates delivery times, and improves overall service quality and client satisfaction. Improved tooling will enable the business to accept more projects, increase recurring contract opportunities, and provide higher-margin packages.

2. Financial & Revenue Goals

The target for Q1–Q3 2026 is a consistent monthly revenue of R35,000. This will cover essential operating costs (AI subscriptions, minor wages), and allow reinvestment to grow equity. Current operations are sustained from personal savings for approximately nine months, while several client projects are expected to generate cash inflow within the next two months. Priority will be given to higher-margin and faster-paying contracts to stabilise cash flow.

3. Market Share & Penetration Plan

Focus on differentiating through creativity, personalised service and competitive pricing. Implement an AI-driven content and SEO programme to increase lead flow from organic search and social platforms. Produce short case studies and client testimonials to convert prospects into recurring clients. Offer bundled service packages (website + SEO + social setup) at attractive entry prices to grow market share.

4. Recovering Losses & Financial Stability Strategy

Adopt a staged investment approach: purchase essential AI subscriptions with the highest ROI first (e.g. SEO automation, code-assist tools). Tighten project selection to prioritise short payment cycles. Improve operational efficiency through automation, standardised templates, and clear scope definitions to avoid scope creep and cost overruns.

5. Future Risks & Mitigation Strategy

Key risks: limited funding, resource constraints (hardware, single developer), and relative lack of in-house AI expertise. Mitigations: start with low-cost/free AI tools and training resources; document processes to reduce single-point dependence; build a small network of trusted contractors to scale capacity as needed; maintain a conservative cash buffer covering at least one month of costs beyond the nine months of current savings.
